Jet platform oxygenator

Pre-sizing of a jet-plate oxygenator: water falls through nozzles into a pure-oxygen gas cushion, the gas passes the compartments in series. Gives nozzles and plate, levels, oxygen transfer efficiency for target outlet O₂, feed gas, vent, total gas pressure and energy. Efficiency is calibrated to the FOX France Oxygénation jet-platform table; confirm on a prototype (vent gas flow and its O₂ %).
